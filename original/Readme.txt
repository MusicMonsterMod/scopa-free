"Scopa (in English: Clean Sweep) is the national card game of Italy and makes for a great two player card game. Scopa Free for Windows is my freeware implementation of this great game. Traditionally, this game is played with an Italian 40-card deck with suits in Swords, Batons, Cups and Coins, and that is how this version is programmed. Note that the Italian deck ranks the Cavall (Knight) below the King, but above the Fante. In version 1.00, I used an international deck stripped down to 40 cards (eights, nines and tens are removed and Jack=8, Queen=9 and King=10). If you wish to play this game when you are not at your computer, you may strip an American 52-card deck to get the resultant International deck.
